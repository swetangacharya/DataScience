we have multiple models that have different levels of accuracy or errors on a given dataset, we can combine their predictions to reduce the overall error and increase the predictive power. patterns and relationships in the data.

### Ensamble Modeling 
Ensemble modeling is a concept revolving around 
the idea that combining the predictions of multiple 
diverse models can lead to better overall performance than any single model in isolation. In simple 
terms, ensemble modeling is a machine learning 
method that involves combining the predictions of 
multiple individual models, called base learners, to 
produce a more accurate and robust prediction
